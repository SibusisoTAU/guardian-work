Privacy Policy - POPIA & ESA Act Compliant

Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 & Employment Services Act 4 of 2014

1. What we collect: Name, phone, town, province, job title, experience. Optional CV data.

2. Purpose: To connect job seekers to verified employers across South Africa. Insights track anonymous views by town to improve service.

3. Legal Basis: Consent via checkbox. Without consent, no profile created.

4. Your Rights (POPIA Section 5): You can access, correct, delete your data anytime. Email: info@myguardianlink.co.za. We delete within 7 days.

5. Storage & Security: Encrypted on Vercel infrastructure (EU/US with SA backup). Access restricted to Information Officer. Never sold.

6. Retention: 3 years or until deletion request. Inactive profiles removed after 12 months.

7. Information Officer: info@myguardianlink.co.za | 0800 222 777 | Pretoria & Secunda

8. ESA Act 4 of 2014 Section 15: We NEVER charge job seekers fees. All job seeker features FREE during growth mode. Report abuse: 0800 222 777 or Department of Employment & Labour.

9. Ads & Insights: We track anonymous ad views/clicks (no personal info) to show advertisers proof of exposure. Town views help us know where jobs needed.

10. Community Reviews: Community timeline is moderated. No hate speech, no scams. Admin can delete.

11. Children: No users under 18.

12. Complaints: Information Regulator South Africa: complaints@inforegulator.org.za

Growth Mode Declaration: My Guardian Link is in FREE Growth Mode (until 20,000 active users). We do not charge job seekers or employers during this phase. After growth, employers pay for premium features only.
